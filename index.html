About Halal Kitchen

Halal Kitchen is a locally owned restaurant located at Rakad Tsamiya-Boka, Hotoro, Kano. We are dedicated to serving fresh, delicious, and 100% halal Nigerian meals prepared with care and quality ingredients.

Our goal is to provide a warm environment where families and friends can enjoy authentic Nigerian flavors at affordable prices.
